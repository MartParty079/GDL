V193 FIBER FIXER

Fiber Fixer remains OFF by default.

When enabled:
1. Each selected image/crop is opened before sweep processing.
2. Dark pores are selected at grayscale 0-40, then the complementary phase is treated as the intact fiber network.
3. The complete fiber phase is skeletonized.
4. The skeleton is expanded to a fixed 3-pixel network.
5. The wireframe mask and original-image preview are saved once.
6. The cached black wireframe is burned into every higher-threshold segmented sweep run.

Completion behavior:
- Always opens the completed output folder in the controller Fiji session.
- Does not auto-open the Word report.
