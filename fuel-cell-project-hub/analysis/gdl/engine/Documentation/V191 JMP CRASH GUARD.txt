V193 JMP CRASH GUARD

Observed failure:
- JMP Student Edition crashed inside WindowMgr.initSwitcher while BEAST had multiple JMP processes active and was tiling JMP windows.

Changes:
- Safe JMP launch mode is ON by default.
- Default parallel JMP processes reduced from 10 to 1.
- Minimum safe launch stagger is 4 seconds.
- JMP window tiling is OFF by default and separated from Fiji/ImageJ tiling.
- Early JMP exits and timeouts are automatically requeued once after a 6 second delay.
- Existing Fiji parallel processing remains unchanged.

You can disable Safe JMP launch mode and increase parallel JMP instances later, but one instance is the recommended stable setting for JMP Student Edition.
